# ============================================================================
# RSBDEFS -- RS_VR_Weapons' own profiles: THE VANILLA LOOK.
#
# These are what the Vanilla and Vanilla+ sets fall back to when their effects
# switch is turned off (`wm_vanilla_look 1`). They are not a lesser version of
# our ballistics; they are a different answer to the question.
#
# WHAT "VANILLA" CAN AND CANNOT MEAN HERE, because the honest limit matters:
#
# Doom's own gun look is a SPRITE. Its muzzle flash is a frame of the weapon
# sprite and its bullet is an invisible hitscan with a puff on the end. Our guns
# draw MODELS and fly a real round, so there is no sprite frame to show and no
# hitscan to borrow -- the switch cannot reach into Doom and pull its look out.
#
# So vanilla mode is what Doom's look AMOUNTS TO rather than how it was made:
# a flash of light at the muzzle and nothing else. No tracer in the air, no
# brass on the floor, no smoke, no shell glint, no kick. The restraint IS the
# look -- in 1993 a pistol shot was two frames and a puff, and a set asking for
# that is asking for the absence, not for a cheaper version of our presence.
#
# THE DAMAGE, THE RATE OF FIRE AND THE ALT FIRES ARE NOT HERE AND NEVER WILL BE.
# The owner, 2026-10-03: a set's behaviour is its identity -- Vanilla's numbers
# are Doom's, Vanilla+ is its own redesign, BD22's are Brutal Doom's -- and the
# switch moves looks only. WM_Sheet refuses a behaviour key inside an `instead`
# block by name, so this file physically cannot change how a gun plays.
# ============================================================================


# ---------------------------------------------------------------- ROUNDS

# NOTHING IN THE AIR. Doom's bullet is a hitscan: it arrives, and the only
# evidence it existed is the puff. `roundlook unseen` draws nothing in flight
# and leaves the impact and the whiz alone, which is exactly that.
#
# THE BALLISTICS ARE `default` AND UNCHANGED. Speed, radius and damage are
# gameplay and are the same in both modes -- this pairing swaps only the look
# half of the round, which is the whole reason a round is two profiles.
round vanilla_bullet
  ballistics = default
  roundlook  = unseen
end


# ---------------------------------------------------------------- FLASHES

# A FLASH OF LIGHT AND NOTHING ELSE. One short, warm light at the muzzle: no
# lit-air cone, no spark bursts, no flame, no smoke, no powder burn, no tail.
#
# It is deliberately not `flash none`. Doom DID light the room when you fired --
# the sprite was bright and the sector flashed -- so an unlit muzzle would be
# less vanilla than this, not more. The light is the part of Doom's flash that
# survives the move from sprites to models.
flash vanilla_gun
  light      = 150, 2.4, 2
  lightcolor = 255, 220, 160
end

# The same, bigger, for a shotgun or a rifle -- Doom's own flash frames are
# brighter on the louder guns and this keeps that one distinction.
flash vanilla_gun_big
  light      = 210, 3.0, 3
  lightcolor = 255, 218, 150
end

# An energy weapon's: Doom's plasma and BFG lit the room blue and green.
flash vanilla_plasma
  light      = 190, 2.8, 2
  lightcolor = 120, 170, 255
end

flash vanilla_bfg
  light      = 260, 3.4, 4
  lightcolor = 150, 255, 160
end
