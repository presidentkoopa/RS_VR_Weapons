Bloom -- the Doom II / Blood crossover, as a weapon loadout for DoomXR.

THIS PACK SHIPS NO ART AT ALL. It carries no meshes, no skins, no sounds and no weapon cards: it
is a player class and a bridge that hand out weapons belonging to RS_VR_Weapons.pk3 and
RS_VR_Weapons_Blood.pk3. Everything it draws is credited in those two packs.

The arsenal and the slot layout were read from Bloom's own Actors/Blood_Weapons.txt rather than
guessed -- its Tesla Rifle is slot 8, its Life Leech slot 9, its Dynamite slot 6, the same slots
Doom's own weapons occupy. Two weapons to a slot is the crossover's design and this reproduces it.

Bloom is by the Bloom team, built on Blood by Monolith Productions and Doom II by id Software.
Nothing of theirs is included here.

NOT REPRODUCED: Bloom carries a Colt and a single-barrel shotgun that this package has not carded.
They are absent rather than faked from a neighbouring weapon.
