RS_VR_Weapons_RTCW -- CREDITS
=============================

THE MODELS IN THIS PACK ARE NOT OURS. Seventeen weapon meshes, used UNALTERED, from

    RealRTCW -- a community overhaul of Return to Castle Wolfenstein (id Software / Gray Matter
    Interactive / Nerve Software, 2001).

What we made is the VR side and nothing else: the hand mechanics, the reload measurements, the
ballistics and the weapon data. The meshes are exactly as their authors exported them. That is the
whole point of the exercise -- these guns have never had a world-model VR treatment, and without
this they would not get one.

Numbers -- damage, rate of fire, magazine size and reload timing -- are read from RealRTCW's own
weapon definition files rather than invented. Their numbers, our names.


PER-ARTIST CREDITS WERE LOOKED FOR AND NOT FOUND
------------------------------------------------

The WW2 set in this family carries all sixteen of its model authors by name, taken from the
community pack's own credits file. THE EQUIVALENT FILE DOES NOT EXIST FOR THESE MESHES. Searched:
the RealRTCW distribution root, its Main folder, and inside z_realrtcw_models_weapons.pk3 and
z_zzrealrtcw_scripts.pk3 -- no credits, readme, authors or licence lump in any of them.

So this is what is known, as far as it goes, and no name is guessed at. A search that ends is a
RESULT and it is recorded here as one. It is not an open ticket and it blocks nothing: this engine
and its mods are private and not for distribution, and an unattributable mesh is fine to use, card,
pack and play.

If a public release of this set is ever decided on, provenance becomes a question about THAT
release -- and this file is the honest starting point for it, because it says exactly what was
looked for and where. Anyone picking it up later should add what they find and still never invent
a name to fill the gap.
