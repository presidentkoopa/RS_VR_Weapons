RS_VR_Weapons_Wardusted -- CREDITS

THE WEAPON MESHES are from "VR Fully Modeled Weapons" for JKXR (Jedi Knight XR), shipped as
z_vr_weapons_jka_Crusty_and_Elin.pk3 -- by Vince Crusty and Elin, per that file's name. The pack
carries no readme or credits lump of its own; that name is all it states, and no other author is
guessed at here. Used unaltered in shape: each mesh is only turned, re-origined at its grip and
scaled to its real weapon's length (_pending/tools/make_sw_sets.py).

THE TEXTURES are Jedi Academy's own (Raven Software / LucasArts, from the owner's retail install),
named by the meshes' own shaders -- the owner allowed the retail textures for the Star Wars sets,
2026-09-21.

THE SOUNDS are from WARDUST's Rogue Rebel (01_WARDUSTs_ROGUEREBEL_10_06_2024.pk3), each gun's own
recording from that mod's SNDINFO, converted to ogg. Its code, sprites and effects are not used.

THE LIGHTSABER is RS_Lightsaber.pk3, loaded separately.
