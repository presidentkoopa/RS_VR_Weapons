Aliens -- a world-model weapon set for DoomXR.

THE MODELS ARE NOT OURS AND ARE NOT ALTERED. They come from the Aliens Eradication VR addon by
Ermac, which is itself an addon to the Aliens Eradication mod by Kontra Kommando and Payload4367.
What this set adds is the VR treatment: each mesh measured into a Model Card so its magazine,
bolt and pump are parts a hand can actually work, and a Weapon Card saying what the gun does. The
meshes themselves ship exactly as their authors made them.

THE SOUNDS ARE THE ERADICATION TC'S OWN, taken from the same mod the models were built for so
they travel under the same credit. Its own CREDITS.weapons is carried in full below, because the
people who made the sounds are not the people who made the meshes and both are owed the line.

NOTHING ELSE WAS TAKEN. No code, no sprites, no class names, no state tables, no muzzle flash or
tracer art. The numbers in WMSHEET.aliens are ours.

Both credits files below are carried from the source packs, unedited.
