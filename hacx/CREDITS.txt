HacX -- a world-model weapon set for DoomXR.

THE MODELS ARE NOT OURS AND ARE NOT ALTERED. They come from a VR weapon pack built for HacX, by Banjo Software.
What this set adds is the VR treatment: each mesh measured into a Model Card so its magazine,
bolt and pump are parts a hand can actually work, and a Weapon Card saying what the gun does.
The meshes themselves ship exactly as their authors made them.

NOTHING ELSE WAS TAKEN. No code, no sprites, no class names, no state tables, and no muzzle
flash, smoke or tracer art -- Blood's flashes are MESHES rather than sprites and are out under
the same rule, because a flash modelled in 3D is still a flash. The numbers in WMSHEET.hacx are
ours.

The source pack's own credits follow, carried unedited.
