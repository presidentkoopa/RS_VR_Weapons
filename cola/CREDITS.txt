Cola 3 -- a world-model weapon set for DoomXR.

THE MODELS ARE NOT OURS AND ARE NOT ALTERED. They come from a GZDoom VR weapon mod by Ermac built
for Cola 3: The Soda of Style by wildweasel. What this set adds is the VR treatment: each mesh
measured into a Model Card so its magazine, bolt and pump are parts a hand can actually work, and
a Weapon Card saying what the gun does. The meshes themselves ship exactly as their authors made
them.

THE SOUNDS ARE COLA 3'S OWN, from the same mod the models were built for, so they travel under the
same credit. Its full credits listing is carried below.

NOTHING ELSE WAS TAKEN. No code, no sprites, no class names, no state tables, no muzzle flash or
tracer art. The numbers in WMSHEET.cola are ours.

ONE NOTE ON THE FIRST FILE: its opening line reads "Robocop Weapons mod for VR", which is the
author's own copy-paste from a sibling pack of theirs -- everything under it is this set's. It is
carried unedited because correcting another person's credits file is not ours to do.

Both credits files below are carried from the source packs, unedited.
